# Information Security Incident Response Runbook

**Classification:** restricted  
**Department:** security  
**Allowed roles:** explicit-grant-only  
**Effective date:** 2026-03-01  
**Version:** 2.0

## Purpose

This runbook defines the initial response and escalation process for suspected information security incidents affecting Northstar Retail Group.

This document requires explicit resource-level authorization. Employee, manager, and CEO role membership alone does not permit retrieval.

## Incident examples

- Suspected unauthorized access to company systems.
- Accidental sharing of confidential or restricted documents.
- Lost or stolen company devices.
- Phishing or credential compromise.
- Unusual database access or data exports.
- Malware or suspicious system activity.

## Severity assessment

- Critical: confirmed or suspected compromise of privileged systems, widespread data exposure, or major business disruption.
- High: unauthorized access to sensitive information or compromise of an important business system.
- Medium: localized incident with limited scope and no confirmed major exposure.
- Low: suspicious activity requiring investigation but with no confirmed impact.

Severity must be reassessed as evidence becomes available.

## Initial response

1. Record the report time and incident reference.
2. Identify the potentially affected systems and resources.
3. Preserve relevant audit records and available evidence.
4. Notify the designated Information Security owner.
5. Limit further access using approved containment procedures.
6. Assess whether credentials, sessions, or permissions require revocation.
7. Document actions, timestamps, decisions, and responsible personnel.

## Evidence handling

Evidence must be preserved in approved restricted storage. Access must be limited to authorized incident responders.

Do not place passwords, authentication tokens, unnecessary personal data, or sensitive evidence in ordinary chat messages or broadly accessible tickets.

## Communication and escalation

Incident updates must be shared through approved channels with authorized recipients.

Escalation decisions must follow the incident severity, business impact, and established response procedures.

## Recovery and closure

Recovery must be authorized and validated. The incident owner must document root-cause findings, impact, remediation, lessons learned, and follow-up actions.

## AI assistant restrictions

The assistant may help authorized personnel summarize permitted incident information or draft documentation. It must not independently grant access, disclose restricted evidence, execute containment actions, or bypass approval requirements.
