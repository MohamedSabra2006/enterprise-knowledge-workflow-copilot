# Employee Handbook — Northstar Retail Group

**Classification:** internal  
**Department:** people-operations  
**Allowed roles:** employee, manager, ceo  
**Effective date:** 2026-01-01  
**Version:** 2.0

## Purpose

This handbook establishes workplace expectations, employee responsibilities, and operational practices to support a professional, respectful, and secure working environment.

## Professional conduct

Employees are expected to communicate respectfully, cooperate across departments, protect company assets, and comply with approved organizational policies. Harassment, discrimination, retaliation, and deliberate misuse of company resources are prohibited.

## Working practices

Employees should maintain accurate project and operational records, follow approved procedures, meet agreed deadlines, and escalate issues that may affect customers, colleagues, or business operations.

## Information security

- Use company-approved applications and storage locations for business information.
- Protect passwords, authentication tokens, and confidential information.
- Lock devices when unattended and report lost or stolen equipment.
- Do not share company credentials or access accounts on behalf of others.
- Report suspected phishing, unauthorized access, or information exposure promptly.

## Information handling

Public information may be shared through approved channels. Internal and business-confidential information must only be shared with authorized recipients for legitimate business purposes. Restricted information requires explicit access authorization.

## Access and responsibilities

Employees must use only the systems, records, and resources authorized for their assigned responsibilities. Managers must not access employee, financial, or departmental information outside their approved scope.

## Security incidents

Employees must promptly report suspected security incidents to the IT service desk or designated Information Security team. Reports should include relevant details without unnecessarily copying sensitive data or credentials.

## AI assistant usage

The internal AI assistant may support document search, analysis, drafting, and workflow assistance. It does not grant permissions or override company policy. Employees must not submit restricted information to unapproved AI services or rely on generated content without appropriate verification.
