# Northstar Retail Group — Company Overview

**Classification:** internal  
**Department:** executive  
**Effective date:** 2026-01-01  
**Version:** 2.0

## Company profile

Northstar Retail Group is a fictional mid-sized retail organization specializing in home, office, and lifestyle products. The company operates an e-commerce platform and 12 physical retail branches across three regions: North, Central, and South.

The organization employs approximately 450 staff across retail operations, distribution, corporate functions, and technology.

## Business model

Northstar generates revenue through online orders, in-store purchases, and business-to-business sales. Its operational activities include procurement, inventory management, fulfillment, customer service, marketing, and financial reporting.

## Departments

- Executive Leadership: corporate strategy, governance, and organizational performance.
- Sales: regional performance, product sales, and commercial partnerships.
- Finance: budgeting, accounting, financial planning, and management reporting.
- Operations: inventory, warehousing, logistics, and order fulfillment.
- Customer Experience: customer support, returns, complaints, and satisfaction.
- People Operations: recruitment, employee relations, training, and HR administration.
- Information Technology: infrastructure, applications, identity management, and technical support.
- Information Security: security governance, risk management, access reviews, and incident response.

## Organizational structure

The CEO oversees department directors and corporate strategy. Department directors manage departmental objectives and resources. Managers supervise assigned teams and operational activities. Employees report to their designated managers.

## Regional operations

- North Region: northern retail branches and distribution operations.
- Central Region: central branches and the primary distribution hub.
- South Region: southern branches and regional sales operations.

Regional performance is monitored through approved sales, inventory, customer experience, and operational KPIs.

## Data governance

Company information is classified as public, internal, business-confidential, or restricted. Access is determined by verified identity, role, organizational scope, business purpose, and explicit resource permissions.

Employment title or seniority alone does not grant unrestricted access to financial records, employee information, customer data, or security-sensitive resources.
